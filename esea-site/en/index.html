Port & Strait Agency

From the Black Sea to the Mediterranean, we manage port calls at every Turkish port and transits through the Istanbul and Çanakkale Straits end to end. Long-standing relationships with authorities, terminals and suppliers allow us to keep your vessels' time in port — and your costs — to a minimum.

Offshore & Energy Logistics

We provide uninterrupted agency and logistics support for drillships, floating production units, semi-submersibles and the PSV/AHTS fleets that serve them, for the full duration of the project. Our field experience in the Black Sea deepwater energy projects is our strongest reference.

Supply, Customs & Logistics

From spare parts and equipment clearance to bunkers, provisions and master's supplies, and from crew changes to project cargo, we coordinate every vessel and site requirement through approved partners.

About Us

ESEA Agency

ESEA Agency was founded in 2016 by a team with over 20 years of industry experience to provide professional agency services to vessels calling at Turkish ports. Having promptly obtained all required licences and its ship agency authorisation, the company began operations alongside its local and international partners.

Since then, we have supported efficient port calls at all Turkish ports and safe transits through the Istanbul and Çanakkale Straits. With the development of the Black Sea deepwater energy projects, we have expanded our agency and logistics services for drillships, floating production units and offshore support fleets.

All planning, management and operational activities are run from our head office in Istanbul. Field operations are carried out by our own teams and approved local partners under our coordination and supervision.
